2. Description of the App

SecureBin is an Android mobile application that uses your device camera and Amazon Bedrock's vision AI to classify waste items into one of eight bin categories, tailored to your Australian state and council. The App is designed as a personal waste sorting aid. Scan results and history are stored locally on your device. A cropped image and your location preference (state and council) are sent to a cloud backend for AI processing. See our Privacy Policy for details.
